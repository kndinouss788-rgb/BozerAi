# S01E01 — Episode Treatment

**Title:** Le monde sans liberté  
**Status:** STORY DIRECTION CORRECTED — opening sequence in development  
**Opening segment target:** approximately 5 minutes  
**Full episode runtime:** not decided  
**Animation generated:** none

> This treatment follows the creator's established premise. Any staging details not explicitly decided remain proposals until reviewed.

## Core premise — CREATOR-ESTABLISHED

In 2050, society is deeply unequal. Powerful people command robot armies and use them to control others. Families are forced to work for the powerful and ordinary people do not live freely.

Aren is the protagonist established in the current character registry. He escapes from his master with an old computer. On that computer is the beginning of an unfinished AI: BOZER. Aren will develop BOZER to help hack oppressive robots and systems, free people and build a small team.

The larger story eventually moves toward human-brain integration with BOZER and abilities beyond ordinary human limits. That is a later arc, not an ability Aren already has in this opening.

## What the opening five minutes must establish

1. **A beautiful world with an ugly injustice:** 2050 should look alive, modern, bright and inhabited. The oppression comes from how people are treated, not from making every street dark and ruined.
2. **The power imbalance:** robots obey human commands. They are instruments of the powerful, not an independent second central AI.
3. **Aren's situation:** he is under the authority of a master and wants to escape. The exact identity and name of that master remain undecided.
4. **The escape:** Aren gets away carrying an old computer. The sequence should feel tense, physical and believable, not like an effortless superhero scene.
5. **The first BOZER encounter:** Aren gets the old computer running and discovers an unfinished BOZER program. At this stage BOZER is conversational; it is not yet a miraculous hacking weapon.
6. **The hook:** Aren sees a possibility: this unfinished AI could become a way to fight the system.

## Tone and visual direction — REQUIRED

- Human, natural character proportions and expressive faces.
- Believable clothes, homes, work areas, streets and family interactions.
- Beautiful modern 2050 architecture: bright, fluid, clean and integrated with mountains, clouds and nature.
- Strong cinematic staging, clear action geography, meaningful pauses and memorable emotional reactions.
- Technology should support the world, not bury it under holograms and robotic decoration.
- Avoid a permanently dark, rusty, industrial, overcrowded robot aesthetic.
- Dialogue should sound like real people, not exposition.
- Action should be powerful but readable and grounded in consequences.

## Continuity safeguards

- BOZER is the protagonist's AI/power in the story, not a deity and never comparable to Allah.
- Robots obey human commands in this world.
- Do not invent a second central AI.
- Do not give BOZER advanced hacking abilities before Aren develops them.
- Do not introduce brain integration or awakened superhuman abilities in this opening.
- Keep the master, city, family relationships and supporting cast details provisional until established.
