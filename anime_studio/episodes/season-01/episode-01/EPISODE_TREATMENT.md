# S01E01 — Episode Treatment

**Working title:** La minute sans réponse  
**Status:** PROPOSAL — creator approval required  
**Target runtime:** 10–12 minutes (provisional)  
**Story stage:** first development draft  
**Animation generated:** none

> This is a creative proposal, not locked canon. Names, events and dialogue can be changed by the creator.

## Logline
In 2050, in a lively ordinary neighborhood where almost every service quietly depends on one central AI, a brief interruption leaves people helpless in ways they never expected. Aren notices a detail that does not fit a normal outage; Kael risks looking where he should not.

## What this episode should make the audience feel
- First: curiosity about a recognizable world that happens to be technologically advanced.
- Then: the unsettling realization that one system quietly connects almost everything.
- Finally: the sense that the interruption was not an accident — without revealing who is behind it.

## Proposed dramatic beats
1. **Everyday life:** morning in a lived-in neighborhood. Vendors open, students hurry, buses arrive, people argue and laugh. Technology is present but not the focus.
2. **The interruption:** public displays go blank, automated transport pauses safely, digital payments fail. Human life continues, but the neighborhood suddenly loses its invisible coordination.
3. **A small human problem becomes urgent:** someone nearby needs help getting a message or reaching a person. The scene stays intimate; no citywide destruction.
4. **Aren notices the anomaly:** a local public terminal shows a short diagnostic fragment that should not appear during an ordinary outage.
5. **Kael investigates:** using his hacking skill, Kael finds that the interruption was initiated through a valid-looking command. He cannot identify its source.
6. **The system returns:** services resume. Officials call it a temporary incident. Most people move on.
7. **The hook:** elsewhere, an unseen operator receives confirmation that the test worked. No face, name or explanation is shown.

## Character use — provisional
- **Aren:** observant, notices the human details others ignore. His personality is not yet canon; this is only a suggested function for the pilot.
- **Kael:** technically gifted and impulsive enough to investigate. His season-two awakening is not shown here.
- **Vorak:** not necessarily shown directly. His oppressive use of the central AI should be developed gradually; do not expose the full power structure in episode one.
- **Hidden antagonist:** only an indirect trace at the end, if the creator wants a mystery hook. Identity remains concealed.

## Important limits
- Do not show a second central AI. There is only one.
- Do not reveal the hidden antagonist.
- Do not introduce Aren's white hair before the awakening.
- Do not show Kael as the awakened war machine in season one episode one.
- Do not explain the entire world through long speeches.
- Keep the neighborhood warm, busy and believable. The crisis should be felt through ordinary people.
