## Continuity notes

- The world is 2050 and should feel modern, bright, lived-in and human.
- Robots obey human authorities; do not portray them as an independent ruling intelligence.
- Families are forced to work for the powerful.
- Aren escapes from his master carrying an old computer.
- The old computer contains an unfinished BOZER program.
- BOZER's first demonstrated ability is conversation / assistance, not instant super-hacking.
- The master's name, Aren's family details, exact city and exact escape route remain undecided.
- No brain integration or awakened superhuman abilities in this opening.
- No central-system outage, hidden operator scene or invented conspiracy hook in this episode opening.
