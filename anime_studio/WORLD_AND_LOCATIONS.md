## World baseline
**Year:** 2050 — CONFIRMED.  
**Central premise:** one central AI coordinates digital services, APIs and intelligent infrastructure across the world — CONFIRMED.  
**Visual rule:** everyday life remains recognizable and human. Technology should feel integrated into homes, streets, transport, work and public spaces rather than turning every location into a sterile sci-fi set.

## Worldbuilding questions
- Which country or countries does season one follow?
- What does an ordinary home look like in 2050?
- What do young people do for school, work and leisure?
- How do people access the central AI in daily life?
- What happens when the central AI is restricted or controlled?
- How does Vorak's oppressive use of the AI affect ordinary citizens?
- What technology is common, rare, illegal or inaccessible?

Keep answers grounded in scenes and characters. Do not add technology just because it looks futuristic.
