# BOZER NEURAL — Anime Studio

This folder is the dedicated production workspace for the BOZER NEURAL animated series. It is deliberately separated from BOZER AI's Android assistant code.

## Mission
Build a persistent, evolving anime-production environment where the story, canon, characters, locations, episode scripts, visual references, storyboards, shots, dialogue and continuity can be developed step by step.

This repository is the source of truth for production documents. External image/video tools are production instruments, not the place where canon is stored.

## Production principles
- Character-driven science-fiction: recognizable human emotions, natural dialogue and believable everyday environments.
- Futuristic elements must serve the story; avoid sterile, excessively futuristic designs.
- Continuity first: approved character and location references are reused across scenes.
- No episode is considered finished merely because isolated AI clips were generated.
- Keep a clear distinction between confirmed canon, proposals and unresolved questions.
- Use affordable/free tools first; never assume a paid plan or credit balance is available.

## Studio modules
1. Canon & series bible
2. Character registry and visual anchors
3. World and location registry
4. Season and episode writing room
5. Storyboard and shot planning
6. Image/video production queue
7. Dialogue, voice and lip-sync
8. Editing, sound and final assembly
9. Continuity review and episode approval
