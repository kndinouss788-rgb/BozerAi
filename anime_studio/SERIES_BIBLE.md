# BOZER NEURAL — Series Bible

**Status:** living document  
**Canon policy:** only facts explicitly established by the creator are marked CONFIRMED. New ideas remain PROPOSALS until approved.

## 1. Identity
- Title: **BOZER NEURAL**
- Genre: science-fiction, action, mystery and drama.
- Setting: **2050**.
- Creative direction: emotionally grounded characters in a recognizable world. Technology can be advanced, but people, homes, streets, cities and conversations should feel natural rather than excessively futuristic.

## 2. Central world premise — CONFIRMED
There is only **one central AI** in this world. It supplies digital services, APIs and intelligent infrastructure used to coordinate the world.

It was created by **Yunus Koanda**, creator code **YK19B**, with the intention of coordinating the world.

**Vorak** recovered this AI and uses it in an oppressive way. Behind the visible power structure, a hidden true antagonist remotely controls it from the shadows.

The exact identity, motives, timeline and methods of this hidden antagonist remain to be developed. Do not invent them as established facts.

## 3. Character canon — CONFIRMED
### Aren
- After the awakening, Aren has **white hair**.
- The precise face, age, build, clothing, personality details and visual design still need creator approval.

### Kael
- Kael's capabilities include hacking, extreme movement speed and powerful aura-like effects.
- After his awakening, he becomes a genuine war machine, capable of devastating destruction.
- In season two, BOZER and Kael become dangers that others feel they must stop.
- Kael is nearly executed, one second from death, when a new **Neural version of BOZER** is installed in his brain.
- The world believes Kael is finished / dead at that moment. The exact aftermath and reveal timing must be written carefully with the creator.

### BOZER
- BOZER is central to the series and is connected to the world's central AI.
- A new Neural version of BOZER is installed in Kael's brain at the near-execution moment in season two.
- The exact relationship between the original central AI, BOZER as a character/system, and the Neural version must be clarified in the story bible before scripting that reveal.

### Vorak
- Vorak recovered the central AI and uses it oppressively.
- Appearance, history, motivations and public role are not yet locked.

### Hidden antagonist
- A true antagonist exists behind the visible conflict and remotely controls the central AI.
- Identity and full plan remain secret and undecided.

## 4. Tone and visual direction — CONFIRMED
- Characters should feel natural and relatable.
- Avoid designs that are so futuristic they become cold, alienating or hard to connect with.
- The world should include believable environments and everyday details, not only laboratories, towers and holographic interfaces.
- Action can be spectacular, but emotional consequences must remain clear.
- Dialogue should sound like people speaking, not exposition machines.

## 5. Continuity rules
1. Never change a confirmed fact silently.
2. Keep proposals separate from canon until approved.
3. Once a character reference is approved, preserve face, hair, body proportions and key costume elements across shots.
4. A character's transformation must be explicitly tied to the correct story moment.
5. Track each scene's time, place, characters, clothing, injuries, emotional state and preceding events.
6. Do not treat AI-generated output as canon until it has been reviewed and approved.

## 6. Open questions — NOT YET DECIDED
- Full main cast and relationships.
- Exact city names, geography, architecture and everyday life in 2050.
- Detailed timeline and political/social structure.
- The central AI's name and precise technical limits.
- The distinction between the central AI, BOZER and BOZER NEURAL.
- Vorak's background and motives.
- Hidden antagonist's identity and objective.
- Season-one episode count, season-one finale and season-two structure.
- Exact designs for Aren, Kael, BOZER, Vorak and supporting characters.
- Voice casting, languages, sound direction and target episode length.

## 7. Approval vocabulary
- **CONFIRMED:** creator-approved canon.
- **PROPOSAL:** an option to consider; not canon.
- **IN DEVELOPMENT:** being written or designed.
- **APPROVED REFERENCE:** visual asset accepted as the stable reference.
- **LOCKED:** cannot be changed without explicit creator decision.
