# Character Registry

This is the canonical index for recurring characters. A character is not visually locked until the creator approves a reference sheet.

## Kael
**Status:** IN DEVELOPMENT  
**Confirmed:** hacking ability, extreme speed, aura-like power effects; after awakening he becomes a devastating war machine. In season two, he is nearly executed and a new Neural BOZER is installed in his brain at the last second; the world believes he is finished.  
**Still needed:** age range, face, hair, build, personality before awakening, ordinary clothing, awakened design, limits/costs of abilities, relationships, voice, exact sequence of the season-two event.  
**Visual anchor:** pending creator-approved reference.

## BOZER
**Status:** IN DEVELOPMENT  
**Confirmed:** central to the story; a new Neural version is installed in Kael's brain during the season-two near-execution event.  
**Still needed:** whether BOZER appears as a physical character, voice, interface, AI presence or a combination; visual identity; relationship to the world's single central AI; voice; limits.  
**Visual anchor:** pending creator-approved reference.
